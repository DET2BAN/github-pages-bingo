🎯 What is BSH Buzzword Bingo?

Buzzword Bingo is a fun game where players mark off buzzwords as they are called out by the organizer. The first to complete a winning pattern wins the game! This edition uses buzzwords from Sherpa Platform, OCO (Order Control), and arc42 Architecture documentation.

🎮 Perfect for: Team events, conference bingo, training sessions, or just for fun while listening to a presentation!

🎟️ Your Bingo Card

Card Layout

Each bingo card has 3 rows × 5 columns = 15 cells with random buzzwords from a pool of 75 words.

The FREE Space

The center cell is always FREE — it counts as marked automatically. This makes the second row (middle row) easiest to complete!

💡 Tip: Focus on the middle row first — you only need 4 more marks since the FREE space is already there!

▶️ How to Play

Step 1: Get Your Tickets

  1. Click Player on the start screen
  2. Enter your name and email/employee ID
  3. Choose how many tickets you want (1, 2, or 3)
  4. Confirm and enter the game

Step 2: Listen & Mark

  1. The organizer calls out buzzwords one by one
  2. When you hear a buzzword on your card, click that cell to scratch it
  3. Your card will show blue highlights when a word is called but not yet scratched by you
  4. The FREE space is already scratched for you
🎯 Pro Tip: Watch for the word announcement at the top — it shows the most recently called buzzword!

Step 3: Complete a Pattern

When you mark cells to complete one of the five winning patterns (see below), a confirmation popup appears.

Step 4: Raise Win Alarm

  1. Click the 🚨 Raise Win Alarm button on your winning card
  2. Select which pattern you completed from the list
  3. The organizer will see your claim and verify it
  4. Once verified, you're recorded as a winner!
⚠️ Important: Only one claim per pattern per ticket. If you claim incorrectly, the organizer will reject it and you can try again.

🏆 Five Winning Patterns

Complete any one of these patterns to win! Each pattern gives you a different medal rank:

🥇 Row 1 (Top) Mark all 5 cells in the top row
🥈 Row 2 (Middle) Mark all 5 cells in the middle row (includes FREE space)
🥉 Row 3 (Bottom) Mark all 5 cells in the bottom row
🎯 Four Corners Mark all 4 corner squares (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right)
🏠 Full House Mark every single square on the card — the ultimate victory!
✨ Strategy: The middle row is easiest (only 4 marks needed)! But many people will go for it. Four Corners and Full House are rarer and more impressive!

🎨 Understanding Card Colors

  • 🔵 Blue highlight = The organizer just called this word, but you haven't scratched it yet
  • ✓ Dark with checkmark = You've scratched this cell
  • ⭐ Gold = This cell is part of your winning pattern
  • ★ FREE = The free space in the center (always counts)

📋 Rules & Fair Play

General Rules

  • You can play 1–3 tickets at once to increase your chances
  • All tickets are based on random draws from the same word pool
  • Only one win per pattern per ticket (you can't claim the same pattern twice on the same card)
  • The organizer has the final say on claim verification
  • If the organizer resets the game, all sessions end and must rejoin

Playing Fairly

  • Only scratch cells for words the organizer actually calls
  • Don't spam claim buttons — raise one claim per pattern per card
  • If a claim is rejected, the organizer will provide feedback
  • Have fun and enjoy the spirit of the game!
🤝 Spirit of the Game: This is meant to be fun! Play honestly, help others enjoy it, and celebrate all winners!

⚡ Tips & Tricks

📢 Listen Carefully — The organizer will announce each word clearly. Watch the ticker at the top for the most recent call.
🎯 Go for Middle Row First — You need only 4 scratches (besides the FREE space) to win. This is the easiest pattern!
🏃 Be Fast — Scratch cells quickly! Multiple people might complete the same pattern. First to claim wins the ranking for that pattern.
💡 Use Multiple Tickets — 2 or 3 tickets double or triple your chances of hitting the same word combinations.
🚨 Know When to Claim — Have your card ready! When you spot a completed pattern, raise the alarm immediately. Don't wait!
🔄 Cross-Tab Play — Open multiple browser tabs to simulate different players. All instances sync in real-time via localStorage!

❓ FAQ

Q: Can I change my mind after scratching?

A: Once scratched, a cell stays scratched. Be careful when clicking!

Q: What if I scratch the wrong cell by accident?

A: Unfortunately, the game doesn't have an undo. Be mindful when clicking. The organizer can help if there's a clear mistake.

Q: Can the organizer declare me a winner without me raising an alarm?

A: No — you must raise the alarm yourself. The organizer will verify your claim and record the win.

Q: What happens if two people complete the same pattern at the same time?

A: First to raise the alarm and get verified by the organizer gets the win. The winners board shows all who completed each pattern.

Q: Can I play multiple games in a row?

A: Yes! The organizer can reset the game at any time, and you can rejoin to get new tickets.

Q: Do my tickets carry over if I close the browser?

A: Your data is saved locally. Returning with the same email will load your existing tickets.
